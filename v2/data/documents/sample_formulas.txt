[1] Classical Mechanics
- Newton II: F = m * a
- Kinetic Energy: Ek = 0.5 * m * v^2
- Potential Energy: Ep = m * g * h
- Work: W = F * d * cos(theta)
- Momentum: p = m * v
- Conservation: m1*u1 + m2*u2 = m1*v1 + m2*v2

[2] Thermodynamics
- Ideal Gas: P * V = n * R * T
- First Law: Delta_U = Q - W
- Heat Capacity: Q = m * c * Delta_T

[3] Calculus Formulas
- d/dx [x^n] = n * x^(n-1)
- d/dx [sin(x)] = cos(x)
- d/dx [cos(x)] = -sin(x)
- d/dx [e^x] = e^x
- d/dx [ln(x)] = 1/x
- Integral [x^n dx] = (x^(n+1))/(n+1) + C

[4] Electromagnetism
- Ohm's Law: V = I * R
- Electric Power: P = V * I = I^2 * R
- Coulomb's Law: F = k * (q1 * q2) / r^2
- Capacitance: C = Q / V
